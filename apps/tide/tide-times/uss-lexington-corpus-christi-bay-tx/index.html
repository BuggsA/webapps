The tide at USS Lexington, Corpus Christi Bay

Pattern
Once-daily
Usually just one high tide and one low tide a day, about 24 h 50 min apart - the pattern of most of the Gulf of Mexico. Form number 5.33.
Typical range
0.6 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.18 m) - a smaller range than 96% of the 235 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.6 ft.
