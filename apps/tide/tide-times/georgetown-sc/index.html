The tide at Georgetown

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.5 ft apart. Form number 0.24.
Typical range
4.2 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.29 m) - a bigger range than 56% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 9 h 09 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
