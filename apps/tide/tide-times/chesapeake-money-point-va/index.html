The tide at Chesapeake (Money Point)

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.4 ft apart. Form number 0.20.
Typical range
3.2 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.98 m) - a smaller range than 52% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 9 h 17 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
