The tide at Lynn

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.9 ft apart. Form number 0.16.
Typical range
9.9 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (3.03 m) - a bigger range than 90% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 11 h 12 min after the moon is highest in the sky.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Massachusetts, 42.458°N 70.943°W.
