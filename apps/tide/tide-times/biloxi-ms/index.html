The tide at Biloxi

Pattern
Once-daily
Usually just one high tide and one low tide a day, about 24 h 50 min apart - the pattern of most of the Gulf of Mexico. Form number 4.60.
Typical range
1.8 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.54 m) - a smaller range than 74% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.5 ft.
