The tide at Haleiwa

Pattern
Mixed, mainly twice-daily
Two highs and two lows on most days, but unequal ones: on average the two highs of a day differ by 0.8 ft. From NOAA’s 701 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
1.5 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (0.47 m) - a smaller range than 80% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
NOAA predicts the tide here from the tide at Honolulu: high water comes 1 h 02 min earlier and low water 2 h 05 min earlier; high tides reach 80% of the height there and low tides 80%.
