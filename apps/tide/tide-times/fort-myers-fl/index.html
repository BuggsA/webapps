The tide at Fort Myers

Pattern
Mixed, mainly twice-daily
Two highs and two lows on most days, but unequal ones. On average the higher high stands 0.4 ft above the lower high, and the lower low sits 0.3 ft below the higher low. Form number 1.15.
Typical range
1.3 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.40 m) - a smaller range than 81% of the 235 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.9 ft.
After the moon
High water comes about 3 h 48 min after the moon is highest in the sky.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Predictions and records: NOAA CO-OPS, station 8725520. Not for navigation or safety.
