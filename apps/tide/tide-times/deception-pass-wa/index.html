The tide at Deception Pass

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 1.59.
Typical range
7.7 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.35 m) - a bigger range than 79% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 4.6 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Washington, 48.415°N 122.652°W. This station has no live gauge: its times are NOAA’s predictions from its harmonic constants. Predictions: NOAA CO-OPS, station 9448614. Not for navigation or safety.
