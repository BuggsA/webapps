The tide at Brandywine Shoal Light

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.9 ft apart. Form number 0.23.
Typical range
5.5 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.68 m) - a bigger range than 71% of the 235 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 8 h 42 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
