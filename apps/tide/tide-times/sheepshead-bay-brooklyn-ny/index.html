The tide at Sheepshead Bay, Brooklyn

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.7 ft apart. Form number 0.18.
Typical range
5.5 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.67 m) - a bigger range than 67% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 7 h 38 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
