The tide at Kwajalein, Marshall Islands

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.7 ft apart. Form number 0.23.
Typical range
3.9 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.19 m) - a bigger range than 68% of the 169 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 4 h 00 min after the moon is highest in the sky.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Predictions and records: NOAA CO-OPS, station 1820000. Not for navigation or safety.
