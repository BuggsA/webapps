The tide at Cameron (Calcasieu Pass)

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 1.57.
Typical range
1.8 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.55 m) - a smaller range than 73% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.2 ft.
