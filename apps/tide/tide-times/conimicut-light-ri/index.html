The tide at Conimicut Light

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.5 ft apart. Form number 0.16.
Typical range
4.6 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.40 m) - a bigger range than 62% of the 235 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 7 h 41 min after the moon is highest in the sky.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more.
