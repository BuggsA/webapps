The tide at Jacksonville (Downtown)

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.1 ft apart. Form number 0.16.
Typical range
2.0 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.60 m) - a smaller range than 69% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 9 h 59 min after the moon is highest in the sky.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Florida, 30.320°N 81.658°W. Predictions and records: NOAA CO-OPS, station 8720226.
