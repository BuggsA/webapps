The tide at Hampton Bays (Shinnecock Inlet)

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.5 ft apart. Form number 0.20.
Typical range
3.5 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.06 m) - a bigger range than 51% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 7 h 12 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
