The tide at Port Aransas

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 2.92.
Typical range
1.0 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.32 m) - a smaller range than 87% of the 235 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.9 ft.
