The tide at Boynton Beach

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.1 ft apart. From NOAA’s 705 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
2.6 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (0.80 m) - a smaller range than 59% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
