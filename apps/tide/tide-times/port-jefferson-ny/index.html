The tide at Port Jefferson

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.6 ft apart. Form number 0.14.
Typical range
7.2 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.18 m) - a bigger range than 76% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 11 h 20 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
