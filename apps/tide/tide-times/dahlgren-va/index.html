The tide at Dahlgren

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.3 ft apart. Form number 0.19.
Typical range
1.9 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.58 m) - a smaller range than 63% of the 235 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 2 h 39 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
