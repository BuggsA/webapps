The tide at Boqueron

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 1.82.
Typical range
0.8 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.24 m) - a smaller range than 92% of the 407 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.7 ft.
