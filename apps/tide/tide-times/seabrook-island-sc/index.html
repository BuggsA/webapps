The tide at Seabrook Island

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.7 ft apart. From NOAA’s 706 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
6.5 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (1.98 m) - a bigger range than 72% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
NOAA predicts the tide here from the tide at Tybee Island (Fort Pulaski): high water comes 0 h 11 min later and low water 0 h 18 min later; high tides reach 90% of the height there and low tides 91%.
