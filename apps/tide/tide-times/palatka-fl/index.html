The tide at Palatka

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.1 ft apart. Form number 0.18.
Typical range
1.4 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.43 m) - a smaller range than 83% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 2 h 51 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
