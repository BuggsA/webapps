The tide at Homer

Pattern
Mixed, mainly twice-daily
Two highs and two lows on most days, but unequal ones. On average the higher high stands 1.7 ft above the lower high, and the lower low sits 3.3 ft below the higher low. Form number 0.28.
Typical range
18.4 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (5.62 m) - a bigger range than 99% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 15.9 ft.
After the moon
High water comes about 2 h 13 min after the moon is highest in the sky.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Alaska, 59.601°N 151.411°W. This station has no live gauge: its times are NOAA’s predictions from its harmonic constants. Predictions: NOAA CO-OPS, station 9455558. Not for navigation or safety.
