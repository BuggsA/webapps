The tide at Provincetown

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.9 ft apart. Form number 0.17.
Typical range
10.1 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (3.07 m) - a bigger range than 90% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 11 h 17 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
