The tide at Southport

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.7 ft apart. Form number 0.24.
Typical range
4.7 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (1.44 m) - a bigger range than 60% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 7 h 43 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
