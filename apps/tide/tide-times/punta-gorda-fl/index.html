The tide at Punta Gorda

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. From NOAA’s 595 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
1.8 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (0.56 m) - a smaller range than 72% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
NOAA predicts the tide here from the tide at St. Petersburg: high water comes 1 h 06 min later and low water 1 h 27 min later; high tides reach 83% of the height there and low tides 83%.
