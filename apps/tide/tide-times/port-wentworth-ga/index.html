The tide at Port Wentworth

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.6 ft apart. Form number 0.16.
Typical range
8.6 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.63 m) - a bigger range than 84% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 8 h 38 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
