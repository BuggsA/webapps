The tide at Bridesburg

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.8 ft apart. Form number 0.19.
Typical range
7.0 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.12 m) - a bigger range than 80% of the 235 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 1 h 41 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
