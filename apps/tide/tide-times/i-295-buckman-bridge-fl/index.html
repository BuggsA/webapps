The tide at I-295 Buckman Bridge

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.1 ft apart. Form number 0.25.
Typical range
1.0 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.31 m) - a smaller range than 88% of the 169 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 11 h 10 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
