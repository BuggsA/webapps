The tide at Berwick (Morgan City)

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 2.25.
Typical range
0.5 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.16 m) - a smaller range than 98% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.4 ft.
