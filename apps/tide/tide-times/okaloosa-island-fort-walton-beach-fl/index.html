The tide at Okaloosa Island (Fort Walton Beach)

Pattern
Once-daily
Usually just one high tide and one low tide a day, about 24 h 50 min apart. Form number 5.20.
Typical range
1.4 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.43 m) - a smaller range than 81% of the 407 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.2 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Florida, 30.391°N 86.593°W.
