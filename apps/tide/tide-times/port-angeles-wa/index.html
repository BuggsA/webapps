The tide at Port Angeles

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 1.60.
Typical range
7.1 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.15 m) - a bigger range than 80% of the 235 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 4.6 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Washington, 48.125°N 123.440°W. Predictions and records: NOAA CO-OPS, station 9444090.
