The tide at Cypremort Point

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 2.87.
Typical range
1.7 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.52 m) - a smaller range than 76% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.3 ft.
