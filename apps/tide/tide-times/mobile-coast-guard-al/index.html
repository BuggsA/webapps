The tide at Mobile (Coast Guard)

Pattern
Once-daily
Usually just one high tide and one low tide a day, about 24 h 50 min apart - the pattern of most of the Gulf of Mexico. Form number 7.20.
Typical range
1.7 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.52 m) - a smaller range than 76% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.5 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Alabama, 30.649°N 88.058°W. Predictions and records: NOAA CO-OPS, station 8736897.
