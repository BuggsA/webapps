The tide at Longboat Key

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. From NOAA’s 616 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
1.9 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (0.58 m) - a smaller range than 71% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
NOAA predicts the tide here from the tide at St. Petersburg: high water comes 1 h 30 min earlier and low water 1 h 17 min earlier; high tides reach 85% of the height there and low tides 111%.
