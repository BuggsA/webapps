The tide at Boca Grande

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 1.59.
Typical range
1.6 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.47 m) - a smaller range than 80% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.9 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Florida, 26.720°N 82.258°W. This station has no live gauge: its times are NOAA’s predictions from its harmonic constants. Predictions: NOAA CO-OPS, station 8725577. Not for navigation or safety.
