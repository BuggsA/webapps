The tide at Bluffton (May River)

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.8 ft apart. Form number 0.16.
Typical range
8.3 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.53 m) - a bigger range than 82% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 8 h 17 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
