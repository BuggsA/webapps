The tide at Brookings

Pattern
Mixed, mainly twice-daily
Two highs and two lows on most days, but unequal ones: on average the two highs of a day differ by 1.4 ft. From NOAA’s 705 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
6.9 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (2.09 m) - a bigger range than 74% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
NOAA predicts the tide here from the tide at Crescent City: high water comes 0 h 01 min later and low water 0 h 04 min later; high tides reach 100% of the height there and low tides 100%.
