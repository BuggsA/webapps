The tide at Sullivan's Island

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.8 ft apart. From NOAA’s 706 predicted high tides of 2027.
Typical range
5.5 ft from the day’s highest high to its lowest low on an average day of 2027 (1.67 m) - a bigger range than 67% of the 501 places on this site.
Predicted from
NOAA predicts the tide here from the tide at Charleston: high water comes 0 h 08 min earlier and low water 0 h 12 min earlier; high tides reach 99% of the height there and low tides 100%.
