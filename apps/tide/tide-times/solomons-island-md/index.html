The tide at Solomons Island

Pattern
Mixed, mainly twice-daily
Two highs and two lows on most days, but unequal ones. On average the higher high stands 0.3 ft above the lower high, and the lower low sits 0.3 ft below the higher low. Form number 0.26.
Typical range
1.5 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.45 m) - a smaller range than 71% of the 169 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.2 ft.
After the moon
High water comes about 1 h 52 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
