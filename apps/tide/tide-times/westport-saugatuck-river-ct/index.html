The tide at Westport (Saugatuck River)

Pattern
Twice-daily
Two high tides and two low tides a day, about 12 h 25 min apart. The two highs are close: on average just 0.7 ft apart. Form number 0.13.
Typical range
7.6 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (2.31 m) - a bigger range than 79% of the 501 places on this site.
After the moon
High water comes about 11 h 17 min after the moon is highest in the sky.
