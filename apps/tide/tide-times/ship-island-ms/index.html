The tide at Ship Island

Pattern
Once-daily
Usually just one high tide and one low tide a day, about 24 h 50 min apart - the pattern of most of the Gulf of Mexico. Form number 5.46.
Typical range
1.6 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.49 m) - a smaller range than 79% of the 501 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.5 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Mississippi, 30.213°N 88.972°W. This station has no live gauge: its times are NOAA’s predictions from its harmonic constants. Predictions: NOAA CO-OPS, station 8744756. Not for navigation or safety.
