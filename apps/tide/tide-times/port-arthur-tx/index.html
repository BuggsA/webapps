The tide at Port Arthur

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 2.48.
Typical range
1.0 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.32 m) - a smaller range than 87% of the 235 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 0.8 ft.

Heights are above mean lower low water (MLLW), the zero of US charts; times are local. The year’s tides are NOAA’s official predictions - wind and air pressure can move the real water a foot or more. Form number: (K1+O1)/(M2+S2) from NOAA’s harmonic constants - below 0.25 twice-daily, above 3 once-daily. Texas, 29.867°N 93.930°W. Predictions and records: NOAA CO-OPS, station 8770475.
