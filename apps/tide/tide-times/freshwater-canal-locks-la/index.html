The tide at Freshwater Canal Locks

Pattern
Mixed, mainly once-daily
Mostly one large high and one large low a day; on some days a small second high and low appear between them. Form number 1.81.
Typical range
2.1 ft from the higher high to the lower low on an average day (0.64 m) - a smaller range than 60% of the 235 places on this site. The mean range, high to low, is 1.4 ft.
